Game Guide & Rules

Map Landmarks

  • Red Circle: Spawn point & Flame Rift. Stand here to heal to full HP!
  • Magic Pond: The glowing arcane pond at the bottom. Approach to buy Charge (20G) and Slay (50G)!
  • Yellow Circles: Arena champions (Ronin, Shinobi, Brawler). Approach to challenge them to a fight!

Coin Clash System (Limbus Company)

  • When you attack, both you and the opponent enter a CLASH!
  • Coins flip: Heads (Gold) increases your Clash Power!
  • The fighter with Higher Clash Power wins the clash and dashes forward to deal DMG!
  • Skills:
    • Punch: 5 Coins | 5 DMG
    • Kick: 7 Coins | 10 DMG
    • Charge: 10 Coins | 20 DMG (Shop: 20G)
    • Slay: 15 Coins | 30 DMG (Shop: 50G)

Controls

Move: W/A/S/D or Arrow Keys or Click / Tap on Map.

Interact: [E] or Spacebar or Tap Interact Button.
